# 🌱 AgriBridge

### Connecting Farmers. Creating Opportunities.

**AgriBridge** is an agricultural platform concept designed to reduce the gap between farmers, market information, and business opportunities.

It brings essential agricultural resources together in one place, helping users explore market prices, discover produce listings, and make more informed decisions.

---

## 🎯 Our Motive

Farmers often face challenges when accessing market information, discovering potential buyers, and understanding the value of their produce.

AgriBridge aims to make these processes simpler by bringing useful information and agricultural tools together in one accessible platform.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Mandi Prices
- Explore sample agricultural market prices.
- View crop price information.
- Visualize market trends.

### 🛒 Agricultural Marketplace
- Browse agricultural produce listings.
- Explore products by category and location.
- View sample quantities and prices.

### 🧮 Smart Tools
- Calculate estimated profit or loss.
- Explore agricultural insights.
- Access information designed to support better decisions.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Explore Information** – Check available mandi price information.
2. **Discover Opportunities** – Browse agricultural produce listings.
3. **Use Smart Tools** – Calculate estimated profit and understand potential outcomes.
4. **Make Better Decisions** – Use the available information to plan ahead.
