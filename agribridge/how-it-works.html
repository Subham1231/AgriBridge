Understanding AgriBridge

A bridge between farmers, information and opportunities.

AgriBridge brings together agricultural market information, selling opportunities and useful tools in one place. Here's how the platform is designed to work and what we aim to build.

Platform Overview

Three ideas. One connected platform.

Every part of AgriBridge is built around three important ideas: helping users access information, discover connections and make informed decisions.

01 / INFORMATION

Understand the Market

Access agricultural price information and explore market comparisons to understand how crops are valued.

02 / CONNECTIONS

Discover Opportunities

Explore a shared marketplace concept that brings farmers and potential buyers closer.

03 / DECISIONS

Make Informed Choices

Use simple tools to estimate profits and understand different agricultural scenarios.

How It Works

From information to opportunity.

The intended user journey through AgriBridge is designed to be simple and accessible.

01

Explore Market Information

Users can visit the Mandi Prices section to explore crop prices and compare illustrative market information.

02

Discover the Marketplace

Users can browse sample agricultural listings and explore how farmers could present their produce to potential buyers.

03

Use Agricultural Tools

Users can explore tools such as the profit calculator to estimate possible earnings based on their own inputs.

04

Make Better-Informed Decisions

By bringing information and useful calculations together, AgriBridge aims to help users understand their options before making decisions.
